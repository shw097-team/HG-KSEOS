# Change: durability gates for lane rounds (epoch 34)

## Why

Six defect classes recurred at least twice each between 2026-10-21 and 2026-10-30, and every round
diagnosed them in prose without changing the substrate. The evidence is in
`config/recurrence-ledger.json` and in `fhs_msc_handoff_20261030/FAR_DURABILITY_ROOTCAUSE_20261030.md`.

## What changes

- `config/lane-routing-registry.yaml` is the single authority for lane routing; the owner order of
  2026-10-24 is in force (`contract_epoch: 34`), PLAN and VERIFY/SECURITY rank 1 =
  `openai-codex/gpt-6.1-sol`, EXECUTE = `opencode-go/deepseek-v4.1-flash`.
- `config/model-lane-assignment.json#lane_chains` and
  `config/lane-route-slots.candidate.json#ordered_pool` are projections, regenerated by
  `scripts/lane_policy_reconcile.py`, which records the epoch digest it projected from.
- `scripts/ir1_policy_gate.py` refuses uncommitted routing policy; `scripts/ir4_subject_gate.py`
  refuses a subject with no commit; `scripts/ir_gate.py` combines them; `scripts/relay_preflight.py`
  refuses a turn whose transport is down.
- `scripts/hgk-lane-dispatch.py --require-durable-gates` runs the gates before any spawn and refuses
  with `ERR_DURABLE_GATE_NO_GO`.
- `scripts/acceptance_default.py` makes the round-closing step mechanical: GRANTED or REFUSED_NAMED,
  never NOT_RUN.
- `scripts/recurrence_gate.py` blocks work on a twice-recurred class that has no regression test that
  names it.

## What does not change

- No model or provider is added, removed or re-ordered beyond the 2026-10-24 order already recorded in
  the registry.
- No lane gains the ability to accept its own work.
- `verify_package.py` and the delivery package remain read-only.
